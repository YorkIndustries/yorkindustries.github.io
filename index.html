Our mission

Under the federal counter-UAS rule, the sites around critical infrastructure may detect drones but may not act, and the agencies newly authorized to act cannot see beyond their own fence. York is building the software that lets whoever detects a drone hand a verified track to whoever is allowed to act on it — in seconds, signed, and verified before anyone acts.

Focus areas

Two sides of one handoff.

Today that handoff is a phone call: minutes late, no coordinates, no way to verify. York's software closes the gap, and the decentralized autonomy work under Army evaluation runs alongside it.

Defense Systems
Counter-UAS airspace awareness with authenticated handoff
6 CFR and 28 CFR Part 124 · SAFER SKIES framework · In development, pilot engagements sought

Software that fuses a site's existing cameras, radar, and Remote ID receivers into one shared, authenticated air picture, holds the track, and hands it to a certified responder who verifies the signature before acting. No new sensors, no central server, no mitigation function: agency personnel act under their own authority and certification.

  1. 1 · DetectThe site's own passive sensors are fused into one air picture.
  2. 2 · HoldEach aircraft is tracked continuously, so the responder gets a live track, not a snapshot.
  3. 3 · Hand off, signedEvery track and status claim carries a standardized post-quantum signature tied to an enrolled sensor.
  4. 4 · RespondThe certified responder verifies the signature and acts. Anything unsigned or altered is dropped.
Minutes → seconds
Design goal: a verified position and heading in the responder's hands in seconds instead of a phone description in minutes. Stated as a target; nothing is demonstrated yet.

Autonomous-systems software
Perception · Coordination · Simulation · Embedded deployment

Perception and multi-target tracking, decentralized multi-agent coordination and task allocation, engagement and mission simulation, and embedded deployment on low-weight compute (NVIDIA Jetson-class). Built for the case where communications are jammed and a node is lost, because that is the case that decides real engagements. The current effort is entirely software and is deliberately structured to remain outside the arms, ammunition, and explosives regulatory regime.

Authenticated by default
